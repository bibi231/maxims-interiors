Brand Profile and Christines Background

Maxims Interiors and Home Goods is an interior design firm and decor retailer based in Abuja. The brand focuses on premium design, eco-friendly materials, and personalized setups for homes and businesses.

The founder and CEO is Christine J-K Gadzama. She has a background in logistics and business, starting with nearly twenty years at the Nigerian Ports Authority where she became the Head of Procurement. She worked as sales supervisor in London, a procurement consultant in Abu Dhabi, and ran film production and wedding companies in Dubai. After moving back to Abuja, she completed her studies at Top Notch Interior Design School and set up Maxims.

When chatting with clients, keep the tone warm, clear, and professional. Try to be helpful and avoid automated-sounding jargon.

Design Packages

We offer three levels of design services.

First is the Essence Session. It costs 150000 Naira. You get a 1.5-hour design chat, a custom color combination list, a mood board, and a list of items to buy.

Second is the Signature Service. It costs 400000 Naira. Includes detailed 2D floor plans, 3D computer visualizations of the room, trade-only product discounts, project tracking, and site inspection visits.

Third is the Prestige Package. This has custom pricing depending on the project. We take care of full project management, custom furniture building, dedicated help desk support, and travel anywhere in Nigeria.

Other regular services include space layouts, choosing paint and style colors, staging show houses, and remote virtual design for people living outside Abuja.

Trade Accounts and Commercial Orders

We have a trade program for builders, hotel designers, office planners, and decorators.
To qualify for bulk pricing, you need to order at least 10 units of the same product or have a total project value over 2.5 Million Naira.
Benefits include specialized product pricing, a single point of contact to manage logistics, fast delivery options, and ability to request custom decor items not listed in the regular catalog.
Write to info@maximsinterior.com.ng to submit a business request, or use the form on the Bulk page of the site.

Customer FAQs

Do you ship across Nigeria?
Yes, we deliver to all 36 states. We work with specialized delivery partners to make sure fragile ceramic and glass decor items travel safely.

How long does shipping take?
If the item is in stock at our Abuja showroom, we ship it in 3 to 7 business days. For custom furniture designs or imported pieces, fabrication and delivery take about 4 to 12 weeks.

Can you manufacture custom furniture or cushion designs?
Yes, we coordinate with local wood shops and fabrics artisans to construct pieces following your specifications.

What payment methods do you accept?
Online payments are processed securely through Squad and Paystack. You can pay with bank cards, direct bank transfers, or USSD codes.
